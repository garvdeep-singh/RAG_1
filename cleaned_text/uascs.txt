TITLE: Uniform Assistance To SC Students
STATE_OR_MINISTRY: Gujarat
TAGS: Financial Assistance, Scheduled Caste, Uniform Assistance

## Details
Objective Main Objective is to provide economic support and motivation for education. Under this scheme, assistance is given to Students studying in classes 1 to 8. Through this scheme, the Gujarat government promotes primary education.

## Benefits
Rs.900/- assistance for three pairs of uniforms.

## Eligibility
The student should be of Scheduled Caste.Students should be studying in classes 1 to 8.No Income Limit.

## Application Process
OnlineApplicants need to visit the official website https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.inNew applicants have to register his/her self first.At the time of registration, applicants have to provide their mobile number, email & set password.Update their profile.Applying for the scholarship.

## Documents Required
Identity proof: Aadhar card, Voter ID, College ID, etc.Residence proof: PAN card, Residence certificate, etc.Income proof.Aadhar card.Passport-size photograph.Academics certificates.Bank details e.g. Account number, IFSC, MICR code.Other documents may be required.Caste certificate.
