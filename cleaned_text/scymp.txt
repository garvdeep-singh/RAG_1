TITLE: Sudama Chhatravrtti Yojana
STATE_OR_MINISTRY: Madhya Pradesh
TAGS: Empowerment, Financial Assistance, Scholarship

## Details
Madhya Pradesh State Government Started Pre-Matric Scholarships for state students. Under this scheme, financial assistance is provided to 300 boys students and 400 girls students of Madhya Pradesh State.

## Benefits
The scholarship amount for male students: Rs. 5000/- annually.The Scholarship amount for female students: Rs. 5250/- annually.

## Eligibility
The parent's annual Income should not be more than Rs. 1 Lakhs.Family Should belong to BPL category.Students should secure above 60% marks in 10th class and currently studying in 11th & 12th class and living in the hostel.Students should belong to the General Category.

## Application Process
OnlineApplicant students have to contact their educational institution.The profile of the students is entered on the education portal www.shikshaportal.mp.gov.in by the teacher of the educational institution as per the record/certificate provided by the student.Fill out the application form and attach the required documents.Submit.

## Documents Required
Residence proof Certificate from residence authority (electricity bill, water connection bill, etc.).Aadhar card.Bank Account Details.Income certificate of parents.class 9th and 10th mark sheet.Minimum 2 passport-size photographs.
