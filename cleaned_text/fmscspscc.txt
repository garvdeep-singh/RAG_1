TITLE: Fodder Minikits under Schedule Castes Sub Plan (SC Category)
STATE_OR_MINISTRY: Gujarat
TAGS: Farmer, Fodder Minikits, Scheduled Caste, Seed

## Details
The scheme "Fodder Minikits under Schedule Castes Sub Planâ€ is implemented by the Directorate of Animal Husbandry, Agriculture, Farmers Welfare and Co-operation Department, Government of Gujarat. Under this scheme, improved varieties of fodder seeds are provided to beneficiaries for widespread publicity, demonstration, and adaptation by farmers.

## Benefits
Under this scheme, the fodder seed minikit of 3 Kg, 7 Kg, or 10 Kg is given to the eligible beneficiary.

## Eligibility
The beneficiary should be a permanent resident of Gujarat.The beneficiary of the Scheduled Caste category can avail the benefit under this scheme.The beneficiary should possess 10 Guntha of land to his or her name.

## Application Process
OfflineTo avail the benefits of the scheme, the scheduled caste beneficiary may contact a nearby Intensive Cattle Development Project Sub Centre.

## Documents Required
1. Identity proof2. Proof of Residence3. Proof of possessing10 Guntha of land4. Any other documents as required
