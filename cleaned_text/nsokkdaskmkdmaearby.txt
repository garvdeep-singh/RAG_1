TITLE: Nirmaan Sthal Par Karya Ke Dauran A-panjeekrat Shramik Ke Mratyu Ki Dasha Me Antyeshti Evan Anugrah Rashi Bhugatan Yojana
STATE_OR_MINISTRY: Madhya Pradesh
TAGS: Construction Worker, Financial Assistance, Labour

## Details
The scheme falls under the purview of the Labour Department of Madhya Pradesh. The scheme provides financial assistance in the form of funeral and ex-gratia payments to the legal heirs of unregistered construction workers who die during construction site work under the MP Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. The deceased worker does not need to be registered with the Madhya Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.

## Benefits
Funeral assistance of ₹ 3,000/-In case of accidental death ₹ 4,00,000/-In case of permanent disability ₹ 2,00,000/-

## Eligibility
The applicant should be a domicile in the state of Madhya Pradesh.Applicant should be a Building and other construction workers.Applicant age should be between 18 to 60 years.

## Application Process
OfflineThe applicant has to submit the application form in the prescribed format and within the time limit to the office of the designated officer.Rural area - Chief Executive Officer, District PanchayatUrban area - Commissioner, Municipal Corporation / Chief Municipal Officer, Municipality / Municipal CouncilApply durationWithin 06 months from the death of the construction worker

## Documents Required
Prescribed application form.Death certificate.Copy of post-mortem report and FIR copy.Disability certificate given by the District Board in case of permanent disability.
