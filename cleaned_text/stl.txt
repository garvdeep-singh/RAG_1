TITLE: Soil Testing Laboratory
STATE_OR_MINISTRY: Meghalaya
TAGS: Agriculture, Farmers, Soil Health

## Details
DescriptionSoil Testing Lab - For analysing soil samples preceding the application of fertilizer in the correct dosage in farmers' field.ObjectivesThe analysis is free of cost of soil samples preceding application of fertilizers/ liming etc. for farmers along with distribution of Soil Health Card (SHC).Mode of Selection of Beneficiary(s)Bonafide farmers have sufficient available cultivable land on a first come first served basis.

## Benefits
To provide soil testing services for farmers free of cost.Soil Health Card (SHC).

## Eligibility
Bonafide farmers engaged in Agriculture

## Application Process
OfflineBonafide farmers can apply in plain paper through the nearest Agriculture Development Officer of a CD Block/Agriculture Circle.

## Documents Required
Farmer's Photo.Verification Form by the Agriculture Development Officer of a CD Block/Agriculture Circle.Bank Details (Account No., IFSC Code) of the applicant.Aadhaar Number (or alternative identification documents.
