TITLE: Safai Karmakar Hetu Aavashyak Upakaran Sahayata Yojana
STATE_OR_MINISTRY: Chhattisgarh
TAGS: Financial Assistance, Sanity Equipment

## Details
Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme "Safai Karmakar Hetu Aavashyak Upakaran Sahayata Yojana" for unorganized worker on 02 September 2015. The main objective of the scheme to empower unorganized worker by providing required equipment for their work.

## Benefits
Registered sanitation workers will receive a grant of ₹ 700/- (Indian Rupees seven hundred only) to purchase essential equipment such as gum boots, gloves, masks, and aprons.Assistance amount will be provided every year.

## Eligibility
Applicant must be registered with the State Social Security Board in any district of Chhattisgarh.Applicant should not receive assistance from any other parallel scheme of the State Government.Applicant should be between 18 and 60 years.
